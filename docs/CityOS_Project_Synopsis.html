1. Introduction

Rapid urban population growth has severely stressed municipal infrastructures across major cities. Municipal authorities struggle to maintain roads, streetlights, drainage pipelines, and solid waste collection. Conventional grievance redressal platforms suffer from severe structural shortcomings: field workers often close tickets fraudulently without physical visits, duplicate reports create database clutter, static first-in-first-out scheduling ignores public safety hazards, and citizens are excluded from the closure verification process.

CityOS is an intelligent, full-stack, GIS-enabled municipal operations platform designed to automate civic problem resolution while eliminating fraudulent work closures through mandatory dual-phase photographic evidence and spatial intelligence.

2. Problem Statement

To design, develop, and validate a secure, spatially intelligent municipal problem management platform that:

  • Enforces mandatory dual-phase photographic evidence (Before-Repair arrival photo and After-Repair completion photo) with detailed resolution logs, locking work order submissions unless visual proof is verified.
  • Eliminates duplicate municipal tickets using a 75-meter spatial proximity clustering algorithm powered by MongoDB 2dsphere indexing.
  • Computes an explainable priority score (0–100) factoring in severity, safety risks, repeat density, and SLA decay.
  • Provides a citizen-centric verification handshake where the reporting citizen inspects side-by-side photographic evidence to "Accept & Close" or "Reject & Reopen" tickets.

5. Key Functional Modules

  1. Citizen Portal (Nagrik Desk): GPS coordinate picking, interactive issue reporting, duplicate linking alerts, and final visual resolution review (Accept & Close / Reject & Reopen).
  2. Nodal Officer Command Desk: Incoming departmental queue (PWD, Sanitation, Water, Electrical), AI classification inspection (94% confidence), and single-click field technician dispatch.
  3. Field Worker Operations (Ground Desk): Work order queue sorted by priority, mandatory arrival verification (Before-Photo), and strict completion locking requiring After-Repair photo and notes (≥10 chars).
  4. City Admin & Commissioner Headquarters: Live executive KPIs, Recharts departmental workload distribution, and geospatial 2dsphere density hotspot monitoring.

6. Mathematical Formulations

Explainable Priority Scoring Formula:
$$\text{Priority Score} = (\text{Severity} \times 0.40) + (\text{Public Safety} \times 0.30) + (\text{Repeat Factor} \times 0.15) + (\text{Aging Decay} \times 0.15)$$ Output range: 0–100. Categorized into CRITICAL (≥80), HIGH (60–79), MEDIUM (35–59), and LOW (<35).
